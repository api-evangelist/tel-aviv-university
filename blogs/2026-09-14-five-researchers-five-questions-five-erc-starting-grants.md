Tel Aviv University leads Israeli universities this year in the number of ERC Starting Grants awarded , with five researchers receiving the prestigious grants from the European Research Council (ERC) . The grants are awarded to researchers at the beginning of their independent academic careers, enabling them to establish research groups and pursue ambitious ideas over several years.
