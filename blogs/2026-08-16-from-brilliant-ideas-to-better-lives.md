The harder challenge is what comes next: taking a promising idea out of the laboratory and giving it a chance to become a technology, a treatment, or a company that can make a real difference. At Tel Aviv University, that challenge is at the heart of the Zimin Institute for Engineering Solutions Advancing Better Lives.
